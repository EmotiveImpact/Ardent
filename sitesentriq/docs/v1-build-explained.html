2 The bug-fix sweep

Before and alongside the new features, we ran several adversarial "try to break it" passes — agents hunting for bugs, other agents verifying each one is real before fixing.

Around 49 genuine issues were found and fixed. The headline ones:

  • Duplicate compliance jobs — the scheduler could create the same recurring (PPM) job twice. Fixed with a race-safe counter.
  • Stale quote-to-job conversions — converting an already-converted quote could double up. Closed off.
  • The "Cell count must match column count" crash — eliminated entirely by moving every table off the engine that threw it.
  • Date / timezone bucketing — jobs occasionally landed in the wrong day on dashboards. Fixed across the app.
  • The sidebar & topbar — the sidebar now stays put when you scroll, and the topbar pins to the top of the screen.

All fixes are covered by the 201 automated tests that run green on every change.

5 The two decisions we parked (and why they can wait)

Neither of these is broken or blocking. They're small choices about how you want things to behave — easy to change after the deploy.

Decision A — When does the customer get their certificate email?

Think of a restaurant: "Performed" = the chef finished cooking. "Completed" = the manager checked the plate before it goes out.

Right now the certificate email only fires on "Completed" — i.e. there's an office check before the customer gets their compliance document. The alternative is to fire it the instant the engineer marks "Performed" in the field (faster, but no review step).

Default we recommend: keep the office check. A compliance certificate is a legal document — you usually don't want a half-finished one auto-emailed. One-line change either way, whenever you decide.

Decision B — How automatic should multi-property invoicing be?

You can already put several properties on one client invoice today — you just type the line items by hand. What doesn't exist yet is a one-click "pull every finished, un-billed job across this client's sites and build the invoice for me" generator.

Why it can wait: the manual route works now. Whether the auto-generator is worth ~1–2 days of build depends on how often you actually bill many sites at once — something easier to judge once it's in real use.
