# SiteSentriq — Positioning Brief

One page every surface (README, marketing site, sales deck, investor memo) should
sing from. Keep it consistent.

---

## Tagline
**Compliance you can prove. Run every building from one place.**

(Alt, shorter: *"The FM platform that makes compliance enforceable."*)

## The one-liner
SiteSentriq is the **compliance-first operating system for facilities-management
operators** — run every building's compliance, maintenance, jobs, contractors and
billing in one place, and *prove* it to any client, auditor or regulator on demand.

## Category
**Compliance-led CAFM for the mid-market** — FM software for operators who manage
*other people's* buildings (managing agents, hard-FM contractors, multi-site service
providers). We sit between three categories that each leave a gap:

| Category | Examples | The gap we fill |
|---|---|---|
| CMMS | UpKeep, MaintainX, Limble | Single-site, compliance-thin, offline app loses data |
| Field-service | simPRO, Joblogic, BigChange, Commusoft | Rigid, weak on portfolio compliance, contract lock-in |
| Enterprise CAFM | Planon, MRI, Vantify | Powerful but slow, costly, 6–12-month consultant rollouts |

## Ideal customer (ICP)
UK/Ireland FM operator, **5–200 buildings under management for clients**, compliance-
critical estates (commercial, healthcare, education, retail, social housing). Feels the
pain of: proving compliance to clients, chasing subcontractor paperwork, SLA penalties,
and an engineer app that loses job sheets.

## The wedge (why we're *us*)
**The rules are enforced by the database, not the screen.**
- Compliance gating — you can't assign a job to a non-compliant subcontractor.
- SLA targets + permits-to-work — enforced at the data layer, not as a reminder.
- Offline-first field capture that **never loses a job sheet** (transactional, replay-safe).
- **One-click proof** — live Compliance Score, public Trust URL, golden-thread Building Pack.

No mid-market tool combines *enforced* compliance + *provable* compliance + a field app that
actually holds up in a basement.

## Proof points (the receipts)
- **Compliance gating** — DB-enforced dispatch block with org-admin override + audit trail.
- **Contract SLA engine** — stamped response/fix targets + automatic service-credit maths.
- **Permit-to-work** — a flagged job can't start without an issued permit.
- **Golden-thread Building Pack** — one-click BSA handover export (HTML + JSON manifest).
- **SFG20-aligned PPM** — plans tagged to SFG20 schedules; the UK credibility marker.
- **Offline-first mobile** — atomic field writes; QR proof-of-presence for SLA attendance.
- **No-login subcontractor work links** — accept → RAMS → photos → sign → submit.
- **Accounting sync you can see + retry** — per-invoice Xero/QuickBooks status + errors.
- **UK cert library** — EICR, CP12, LOLER, L8 legionella, BS 5839 fire alarm, and more.
- **Continuous Compliance Score + public Trust URL** — compliance as a sales asset.

## The three go-to-market weapons (from the competitive research)
1. **Anti-lock-in** — monthly rolling terms + one-click full data export ("test the exit").
   The incumbents are notorious for exit fees and data-hostage complaints.
2. **All-features pricing** — no per-user paywalls on offline mode, reporting, or work orders.
3. **Live in a day** — self-serve onboarding + CSV import vs. 6–12-month CAFM rollouts.
