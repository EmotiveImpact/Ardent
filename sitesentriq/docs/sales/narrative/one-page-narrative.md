## The problem

UK facilities-management operators run a compliance treadmill across 12+ regulatory regimes — Gas Safe, NICEIC, LOLER, Legionella, fire alarm servicing, emergency lighting testing, PAT, fixed-wire, asbestos surveys, water hygiene. Multiplied across multiple clients and dozens of sites.

The work itself is not the hard part. **Proving the work is.** When a top client, an auditor, an insurance broker, or a regulator asks *"are you compliant?"*, the honest answer for most operators is "let me get back to you" — followed by two days of chasing engineers for certificates, hunting through shared drives, and assembling spreadsheets.

That assembly cost is invisible on the P&L but real. It also creates the worst kind of risk — the gap between *being* compliant and *being able to prove* it. Operators rarely fail on the doing; they fail on the evidence.

---

## The shift other industries already made

Cybersecurity moved from on-demand audits to continuous monitoring (Vanta, Drata). Accounting moved from quarterly close to real-time bookkeeping (Brex, Pleo). Logistics moved from "where's my parcel" to live tracking (Onfleet, Bringg).

UK facilities management has not made this shift. Every CMMS we've benchmarked — Joblogic, Trackplan, Pirana, FM360 — still treats compliance as a per-asset traffic-light report you generate on demand. None of them produce a continuous, org-level compliance number or a public verification surface.

## What SiteSentriq does differently

We built three things no other UK FM platform has:

**1. Continuous Compliance Score.** A single number, 0–100, calculated nightly across every site and every client. Composed of compliance status (40%), PPM on-time performance (25%), job pass rate (25%), and open critical action health (10%). It updates while you sleep. You see your score on the dashboard the moment you log in.

**2. Public Trust URL.** Every organisation gets a public, no-login page — `sitesentriq.com/trust/{your-slug}` — where insurers, top clients, auditors, or your insurance broker can verify your live score and compliance status without you sending them anything. Shareable in a proposal. Citable in a tender response. Standing proof of your operational discipline.

**3. Monthly Statement of Compliance.** Every first of the month at 06:00 UTC, a branded PDF lands in your organisation's support inbox covering the previous calendar month's score, breakdown, and headline activity. Auditable trail. Insurance-grade evidence. Zero effort.

## And everything you'd expect

The rest of the platform is a full FM CMMS — PPM scheduling, reactive job dispatch, mobile field engineer app (Expo, offline-queue mutations, photo capture with EXIF compression, signature pads, GPS check-in), client portal (raise call-outs, view jobs, download service reports), site portal (per-tenant subdomain, white-label header), purchase orders, supplier catalogue, contracts/SLAs, quote → job → invoice workflow, engineer accreditation tracking, multi-tenant data isolation enforced at the database level via Postgres Row-Level Security, real-time updates broadcast across browsers and devices.

The Continuous Compliance Score is the headline. The rest is the foundation that makes the score trustworthy.

- Hold contracts with regulated or insurance-sensitive clients (NHS, councils, retail, hospitality, regulated landlords)
