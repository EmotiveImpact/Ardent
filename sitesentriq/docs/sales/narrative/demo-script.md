# Demo Script — 20 Minutes

The structured second-call walkthrough. Designed for prospects who have already had a discovery call (`discovery/interview-guide.md`) and have asked to see the product.

**Critical rules**
- Voice/video, screen-shared. Never slides.
- 20 minutes hard ceiling — they have other meetings.
- Drive the prospect's actual scenario into the demo (the one they described in discovery) — not a generic walkthrough.
- End with a specific next-step ask, not "what do you think?"

---

## Pre-call setup (15 min before)

- Open these tabs in the order they'll appear:
  1. **Dashboard** — pre-logged-in as a seeded demo org with realistic data
  2. **Trust URL** — public, in a separate browser profile (so it loads as "no-login")
  3. **Job detail page** — one mid-lifecycle job you'll walk through
  4. **Mobile app** — running on an iPhone simulator or real device, mirrored to screen
  5. **Statement of Compliance PDF** — one example open in Preview
  6. **Pricing page or note** — the one-pager pricing line ready to share

- Close everything else. No Slack, no Notion, no terminal. They watch one thing.

- Have your discovery-call notes open in a separate window — refer to their specific pain points by name.

---

## The structure

| Minutes | Section | What you're doing |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Context | Confirm what they told you on call one |
| 2–6 | The score moment | Open dashboard. Show the score. Show the breakdown. |
| 6–9 | The trust URL | Open the public URL. Send them the link mid-call. |
| 9–13 | Job lifecycle | Quote → schedule → mobile field → photos → signature → invoice |
| 13–15 | The artefacts | Statement of Compliance PDF. ICS calendar feed. |
| 15–18 | Pricing + next step | Specific number, specific contract, specific ask. |
| 18–20 | Their questions | Listen, write, don't defend. |

---

## 0–2 min — Context (don't skip)

> *"Quick recap from last week — you told me [their specific pain point, verbatim]. You also mentioned [the moment of pain — specific incident]. And you were curious about [whichever differentiator they reacted to]. I'm going to walk you through how the platform handles all three. If we go off-piste or you want to dive into anything, just stop me."*

Why this matters: signals you actually listened. Makes the rest of the call feel personalised, not template. Saves you 5 minutes you'd otherwise spend re-establishing context.

---

## 2–6 min — The score moment

Open the dashboard.

> *"This is what you see the moment you log in. The big number top-left is your live compliance score for your whole organisation — every site, every client, every regime — calculated last night."*

Pause. Let them look. Don't keep talking.

Click into the score breakdown.

> *"Four components: compliance status — what percentage of your statutory items are current. PPM on-time — how reliably you completed planned maintenance in the last 90 days. Pass rate — how many of those jobs passed first time. Action health — open critical defects older than 7 days. Weighted 40/25/25/10. Updates nightly, every org, automatically."*

If they ask "how is X calculated" — show them. The transparency is the trust.

If they ask "what if I disagree with the score" — *"You can manually trigger a recompute right here. Or just wait — tonight's cron picks up new data. The point is the score is always grounded in your actual operational data, not on you remembering to update something."*

If they're quiet, prompt: *"Does this map to how you'd think about your compliance health, or would you weight it differently?"* — their answer is gold.

---

## 6–9 min — The trust URL (THE moment)

> *"Now the bit nobody else in UK FM has. Watch this."*

Open the public Trust URL in the separate browser profile.

> *"This is a public page. No login required. Anyone with the link can see it. The score, the breakdown, the last-updated timestamp, the organisation logo, contact info — all verified live from our database, no caching, no spoofing."*

Pause for visible reaction.

Then — and this is the move — paste the URL into the call chat. Say:

> *"Open it on your phone while I'm talking. Refresh it. That's a real, live, verified score for [demo org]. Imagine that URL was yours and you sent it in a tender response, or your insurance broker bookmarked it, or your top client had it on speed-dial."*

While they're playing with it, talk through the use cases:

- Embed it in proposals: *"watch our compliance health in real time"*
- Send to insurers at renewal: lower premiums for verifiable risk reduction
- Include in tender responses: proof you're not just claiming compliance
- Use in regulatory submissions: timestamped, auditable, third-party verifiable

Common reaction: *"Wait, why doesn't [Joblogic / Trackplan / their current system] have this?"* — and the honest answer is *"They could, eventually, but their architecture is per-asset reporting. Ours is org-level continuous monitoring. Different product philosophy."*

---

## 9–13 min — Job lifecycle (the foundation)

Now anchor it. Show that the score isn't just a chart — it's grounded in real workflow.

Walk through one job, ideally one similar to their world:

1. **Quote.** Show a draft quote with markup, line items, customer-facing template. *"Two seconds to convert to a job once approved."*
2. **Schedule.** Drag it onto an engineer's calendar. *"Real-time updates push to their mobile app instantly."*
3. **Mobile.** Switch to the mobile screen. Field Mode opens, shows the day's jobs. Open the assigned one. *"Engineer hits 'travelling' — GPS captured. Arrives on-site — auto check-in. Completes work — photos, signature, parts used, all captured offline if signal's bad."*
4. **Photos / signature.** Show one or two photos uploading. Show the signature pad.
5. **Back to web.** Show the completed job — photos visible, signature on the service report, status updated, score recalculating.

> *"That whole loop closes the audit trail. When your top client asks for proof of work, every job has GPS, timestamps, photos, signature, parts, engineer accreditation at time of work — all in one click."*

Avoid feature-listing temptation. Show ONE job end-to-end, not five features.

---

## 13–15 min — The artefacts

Open the Statement of Compliance PDF.

> *"Every first of the month at 06:00 UTC, one of these lands in your support inbox. Last month's score, breakdown, headline activity. Branded, dated, signed. Send it to your board, your insurer, your big client. Zero effort."*

Show the ICS calendar feed.

> *"And every org gets a subscription URL — paste it into Google Calendar or Outlook and your upcoming PPM, jobs, and engineer schedules appear in your normal calendar. Your engineers don't even need to open the app to see what's coming."*

These two artefacts together = unfair advantage in a tender response. Lead with that.

---

## 15–18 min — Pricing + next step

Direct, no waffle:

> *"Pricing for your size of operation is £[X]/month. Includes everything I've shown — Trust URL, statement PDF, mobile app, all integrations, support. Annual contract with a month-one satisfaction clause — if it doesn't work for you in the first 30 days, full refund, no questions."*

Pause. Don't fill silence.

> *"Two specific next steps I'd suggest. First — I create you a real org on the platform, seeded with two or three of your actual sites, and you spend a week using it for free. End of week we book a 15-minute call — you tell me yes, no, or 'almost but X needs to change'. Or second — if you'd rather see it run on your full operation, we can do a paid pilot, 90 days at £[X], full money back if you don't want to continue. Which fits better for you?"*

The two-option close avoids the "let me think about it" trap. Both options say yes. The "no" option is theirs to volunteer.

If they say *"let me think"*: *"Totally fair. Can I check in next [day] at [time]? I'll just have one specific question for you — whether the trust URL was useful enough to warrant a closer look."*

---

## 18–20 min — Their questions

Shut up. Let them talk.

Take notes verbatim. Don't defend. Don't explain. If they raise an objection you can't answer in 30 seconds: *"Genuine question — let me think about that properly and come back to you tomorrow. I'd rather give you a real answer than a fast one."*

End with:

> *"Thank you. Genuinely useful — even if we don't end up working together, watching someone in [their segment] interact with the trust URL just told me something about the product. I'll [book the trial / send the pilot proposal / circle back Thursday] today."*

---

## After the call

### Within 1 hour
- Send the next-step asset (trial seed link / pilot proposal / follow-up time confirmed)
- Update `docs/sales/findings/round-1-call-N.md` with their reaction to each section

### Within 24 hours
- If they signed up for a trial: send a 3-line welcome message with one specific thing to try first (usually: "play with your trust URL, refresh it after I add a sample compliance item")
- If they're considering: pencil a follow-up

### If they say no
- Send a warm thank-you. Ask the killer question: *"What would have to be different about the product for you to say yes?"* — they'll answer honestly post-call.
- Add to the "circle back in 6 months" list.
- Don't grieve. One no is one data point in a 5-call round.

## Common questions + answers

**"How long has this been built?"**
*"The core platform has been in development across 2025-2026. Continuous Compliance Score shipped early May. Trust URL shipped May. We're in customer-discovery phase — talking to operators like yourself before scaling."*

**"How many customers do you have?"**
*"Being honest with you — you're one of the first operators we're showing it to. We're prioritising getting the product right with real operators over racking up logos. If that's a dealbreaker, tell me now and we'll part as friends."* (Vulnerability beats false claims. Operators respect honesty.)

**"What if you go bust?"**
*"Fair question. Your data is on Supabase, EU-West-2. Our GDPR export endpoint will hand you a complete dump of every table you own at any time, no questions asked. You're never locked in. We've also published our score-calculation algorithm — you could reimplement it yourself in a weekend if you wanted to."*

**"Can you integrate with [their existing system]?"**
*"For round one we're focused on operators who'd use SiteSentriq as their primary. If you'd want it to coexist with [system], let's talk about what specifically — sometimes a CSV nightly export from us into them is enough; sometimes we'd need a real-time webhook. What's the use case?"*

**"What about [specific compliance regime they raised]?"**
- If we have it: show them on screen.
- If we don't: *"Not yet — but it would be a 1-2 day add given how the compliance module is structured. Would that be a dealbreaker, or a 'nice to have within 90 days' thing?"*

**"Can I try it for a week?"**
*"Yes — give me your email and I'll have you logged in within an hour, with a seeded version of your operation. Five free sites. Trust URL live."*
