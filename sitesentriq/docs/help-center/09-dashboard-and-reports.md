# Dashboard & reports

## Your dashboard

The dashboard is your home screen — a greeting, your compliance score, operational KPIs,
trends and recent activity.

### Customise it

Click **Customise** on the dashboard to:

- **Show or hide** any widget.
- **Reorder** widgets within their area (the full-width section, the feed column, the
  activity rail).
- **Reset** to the default at any time.

Your layout is saved to *your* account — every user can arrange their own. When we add a
new widget, it appears automatically.

## The report builder

Build reports **without writing SQL**:

1. Go to **Reports → Builder**.
2. Pick what you're reporting on and apply filters.
3. Run it, export it (CSV), or save it as a template.

## Scheduled reports

Save a report as a **schedule** and have it emailed to you (or your client) automatically —
daily, weekly or monthly — as a CSV attachment or inline HTML. The schedule runs in the
background and shows you its last-run / next-run dates.

## Analytics

The analytics area gives you charted views on real data — jobs, compliance, trends — to
spot what needs attention.
