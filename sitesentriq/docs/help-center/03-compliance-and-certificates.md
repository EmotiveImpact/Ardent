# Compliance & certificates

Compliance is the backbone of SiteSentriq. The product is built so you can not only
*track* compliance but *demonstrate* it.

## Compliance items

Each property/asset has **compliance items** — the statutory checks it needs (fire alarm,
emergency lighting, gas safety, etc.) with a **frequency**, a **last-done** date, a
**next-due** date and a **status** (Compliant / Due soon / Overdue / Non-compliant).

The compliance area shows you what's coming up and what's overdue across the portfolio.

## The Compliance Score

Every organisation has a live **Compliance Score** (0–100, banded) calculated from your
real data — compliance status, planned maintenance, job pass rate and remedial actions.
It's your at-a-glance health number, and it trends over time.

## Public Trust URL

You can publish a **public Trust page** — a shareable, no-login URL that shows your
compliance standing. It turns compliance into a sales asset: send it to a prospect or put
it on a tender. Interested parties can subscribe to be alerted.

## Statement of Compliance & Tender Pack

- **Statement of Compliance** — a branded PDF summarising your compliance position.
- **Tender Pack** — a one-click compliance evidence bundle for bids (score + trend +
  in-date certificates + compliance summary).

## Certificates (engineer checklists)

Engineers complete **certificate checklists** on the [mobile app](./10-mobile-app.md). The
**UK statutory library** includes:

- **EICR** (BS 7671) — electrical installation condition
- **Gas Safety Record / CP12** (Gas Safe)
- **Emergency lighting** (BS 5266)
- **Fire alarm** — weekly test and quarterly service (BS 5839)
- **LOLER** — lifting equipment
- **Legionella** — monthly and quarterly checks (ACoP L8)
- Plus fire extinguisher, fire & alarm system, and access control

Each is a guided checklist (pass/fail items plus readings and a faults/observations note).
Completed certificates attach to the job and show on the property record.

> These are the **engineer checklist** certs. Printable certificate **documents**
> (formatted EICR / gas-safety / fire-risk PDFs) are a separate output.
