# The client portal

Give your clients their own login to see the work you do for them — without phoning your
office. It's a trust and transparency feature, and it cuts down "where's my…?" calls.

## What a client can do

- **Switch between their sites** — a multi-site client picks the property they're looking at.
- **See visits** — upcoming and completed jobs at their sites.
- **See compliance** — the compliance status of their properties.
- **View documents** — the documents you've shared with them.
- **View invoices** — and invoice detail.
- **Approve quotes** — approve or reject a quote you've sent, right in the portal.
- **Raise a call-out** — report a problem at their site, with photos, which lands as a job
  in your system and notifies your ops team.
- **Portfolio view & contact** — an overview across their estate, and how to reach you.

## On-site staff (the site surface)

Beyond the client admin, on-site staff at a property can have a scoped **site surface** —
they see their own property's visits, compliance and documents, and can raise call-outs,
without seeing anything from other clients or sites.

## How access works

Clients are invited by email and scoped to their own client/properties only — they can
never see another client's data. The same row-level security that protects your
organisation's data protects each client's view.
