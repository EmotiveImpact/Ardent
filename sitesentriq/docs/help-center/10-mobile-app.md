# The engineer mobile app

The mobile app is built for field engineers — and built to work **even with no signal**.
Nothing you capture in the field is lost.

## Works offline, never loses your work

Every action you take — checking in, adding a photo, a signature, parts, a certificate,
marking failed access — is saved on the device and synced when you get signal back. If
you're in a basement or a rural site, just keep working; it uploads later. You'll see a
pending/synced indicator so you know where things stand.

## Working a job

Open a job from **Today** or your job list. The job screen is a hub of actions:

- **Check in / out** — records your arrival (with location) and start of work.
- **Photos** — take and attach photos (compressed automatically).
- **Parts** — log materials used, with quantities and costs.
- **Time** — record time on the job.
- **Notes** — add a note for the office.
- **Certificate** — complete a [statutory certificate](./03-compliance-and-certificates.md)
  (EICR, CP12, LOLER, Legionella, fire alarm, etc.).
- **Defect** — raise a defect/follow-up.
- **Sign-off** — capture customer and engineer signatures.
- **Documents** — view job documents.

## QR proof-of-presence

Tap **QR check-in** and scan the site or asset QR code. This records hard **proof you were
on site** — and it automatically satisfies the SLA "responded/attended" target for the
job. It shows on the office's job timeline as "Proof of presence". It works offline too.

## Failed access

Can't get in? Use the **failed access** action and pick a reason. The status and the reason
are saved together as one record — so the office always sees *why*, never a status with no
explanation.

## Permit-to-work jobs

If a job needs a [permit](./06-permits-to-work.md), you'll see a banner. If none is issued,
check-in is blocked with a clear message and you can **Request a permit** from the field —
the office then issues it.

## Live location

When you check in, you can share your live location so the office's Engineer Tracker shows
where you are — handy for dispatch and for the customer's ETA link.
