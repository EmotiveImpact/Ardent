# Getting started

SiteSentriq helps facilities-management operators run **every building's compliance,
planned maintenance, reactive jobs, contractors, quotes and invoices from one place —
and prove it.**

## The core model

Your data is organised as a hierarchy:

**Clients → Properties → Assets**

- A **client** is who you bill (a managing agent, a landlord, a business).
- A **property** is a building or site you look after for that client.
- **Assets** are the equipment in a property (boilers, fire panels, lifts…).

Work hangs off this: **compliance items**, **maintenance plans**, **jobs**, **documents**
and **contracts** all attach to a property or asset.

## The four kinds of user

| User | Where they work | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Office / admin** | Web dashboard | Set rules, schedule work, raise quotes/invoices, manage clients |
| **Field engineer** | Mobile app | Do the visits — check in, photos, signatures, parts, certificates |
| **Subcontractor** | A no-login link | Accept one job, upload RAMS, submit work |
| **Client** | The portal | See their sites, compliance, visits, quotes, invoices |

## Roles & permissions

Office users have roles that control what they can see and do — typically
**org admin**, **scheduler**, **finance**, **engineer**, **auditor**. Some actions are
restricted (for example, only an admin or scheduler can **issue a permit** or **override**
a blocked subcontractor assignment).

## The golden rule of the product

**The office sets the rules; the database enforces them.** Compliance requirements,
SLA targets and permit gates aren't just screen warnings — they're enforced in the
backend, so they can't be skipped by accident. The rest of these guides show you how to
set each one up.

## Finding your way around

- The **dashboard** is your home — KPIs, compliance score, recent activity. You can
  [customise which widgets show](./09-dashboard-and-reports.md).
- The left sidebar takes you to Clients, Properties, Assets, Compliance, Maintenance,
  Scheduler, Jobs, Quotes, Invoices, Documents, Reports, Subcontractors, Contracts and Settings.
- **Settings** is where the rules live — including [Subcontractor compliance](./04-subcontractors.md)
  and [SLA policies](./05-sla-and-contracts.md).
