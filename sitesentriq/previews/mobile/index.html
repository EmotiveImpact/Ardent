Tradeoffs at a glance

01 · iOS Light + Most native, ages well, lowest build risk, accessible contrast.
− Least distinctive; brand recedes; can read as "plain".
02 · Field Bold + Best glanceability outdoors, large targets for gloves, strongest brand chrome.
− Heavy/dark all-over; least "iOS-stock"; dark UI needs care for long sessions.
03 · Vibrant Cards + Friendliest, most memorable, great for onboarding & marketing screenshots.
− Gradients & shadows can feel busy; furthest from HIG; more design upkeep.

Recommendation: 01 is the safe, native default the design review pushed toward (large titles, reduced navy, brand-as-accent). 02 wins if field conditions (sunlight, gloves) dominate. A strong hybrid: ship 01's structure (large titles, grouped lists, neutral chrome) with 02's bigger action row + status spines on the Job Detail hero, borrowing 03's warmth only on the Today greeting.
