+ Product

Every job, crew and certificate on one screen.

01 Dispatch

Plan the day, then watch it happen.

Every engineer, every van and every job on one board. Drop work into a gap, see who is running late, and let Ardent suggest the nearest engineer with the right tickets.

  • Drag and drop jobs across the day
  • Late arrivals flagged before the customer rings
  • Unassigned work waits in one queue

02 Live job sheet

Know where every job stands.

The job sheet fills itself in as the engineer works: arrival, photos, readings, sign-off. The office sees the same record in real time, with no phone calls to chase it.

  • Booked, en route, on site, done, invoiced, paid
  • Site, window and crew on one card
  • Notes and photos stamped with time and place

03 Compliance

Mistakes get stopped, not discovered.

Compliance gates sit inside the job. An engineer with a lapsed registration can’t be dispatched, and a gas job can’t close without its tightness test.

  • Gas Safe, NICEIC and NAPIT numbers checked on dispatch
  • RAMS signed before work starts
  • Exceptions logged and chased automatically

04 Certificates

One register for every certificate you issue.

CP12s, EICRs, PAT, fire alarm and unvented certificates are issued from the job and filed against the property. Renewals are booked before they lapse, not after.

  • Overdue, due soon and valid at a glance
  • Renewal reminders sent to landlords and agents
  • Every certificate a click away from the property

05 Money

Get paid for the work you’ve done.

Quotes turn into jobs and jobs turn into invoices without retyping. You see what is owed, who is late and how this week compares to last.

  • Quotes, invoices and part payments
  • Overdue invoices chased for you
  • Syncs with Xero and QuickBooks

06 Crew on the road

Every van, live.

See who is on site, who is en route and who is free, with arrival times worked out from real traffic.

  • Live status for every engineer
  • Arrival times shared with customers
  • Nearest free engineer for emergencies
