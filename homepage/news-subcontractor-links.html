Product update: subcontractor job links now collect RAMS and signatures with no login

Notes from our field team on the first 500 jobs sent to subcontractors through a single link.

18 / 09 / 20263 min readArdent product team

Subcontractors do a big share of the work for many of our customers, but asking them to download an app and create an account never worked. So we stopped asking.

What is new

  • Send any job to a subcontractor as a secure link by text or email.
  • They read the RAMS, sign them, and upload photos and certificates from their phone browser.
  • Everything lands on the job in Ardent, exactly as if your own engineer had done it.

What we learned from the first 500 jobs

Most links were opened within minutes, and the biggest win was not speed but completeness: jobs came back with the photos and paperwork attached, instead of a promise to send them later.

Job links are available now on the Crew and Fleet plans.
