What Ardent handles for electrical businesses

  1. 01

    Certificates from the job

    EICs, EICRs and Minor Works certificates are filled in from the job sheet, with schedules of test results captured on the phone.

  2. 02

    Landlord inspections on repeat

    Every EICR carries its next due date. Ardent books the re-inspection and reminds the landlord or agent before it lapses.

  3. 03

    Observations that turn into work

    C1, C2 and FI codes on a report become a quote in one tap, so remedial work is priced while you are still on site.

  4. 04

    Scheme audits without the panic

    Every certificate, photo and test result is kept against the job, ready for your NICEIC or NAPIT assessor.
