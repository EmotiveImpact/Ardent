Run the job.

Ardent brings jobs, certificates, quotes and invoices together, so engineers and the office are always working from the same page.

+ Our focus

Ardent was shaped alongside electricians, gas engineers and building services contractors, with one aim: less time chasing paperwork and more time on the tools, with every certificate, photo and signature filed the moment the job is done.

From the first call to the final invoice, every job carries its own record. Engineers see what they need on site, the office sees progress as it happens, and clients get proof without asking twice.

Compliance sits inside the workflow. Expired accreditations block dispatch, permits gate the start of work, and gas, electrical and fire certificates are issued from the job itself.

Your kit

  1. [01]

    Jobs & scheduling

    Drag jobs onto the right engineer, with skills, travel and availability in view. Reactive call-outs and planned maintenance share one board, and SLA timers count down on every job.

  2. [02]

    Certificates & compliance

    Issue EICRs, CP12s, fire alarm and emergency lighting certificates straight from the job. Renewals are tracked per property, and a live compliance score shows clients exactly where they stand.

  3. [03]

    Quotes & invoicing

    Turn a site survey into a priced quote, convert it to a job when the client approves online, and invoice the moment work is signed off. Xero and QuickBooks sync, with every push visible and retryable.

  4. [04]

    Engineer app

    An app that keeps working in basements, risers and plant rooms. Photos, readings and signatures save on the device and sync when signal returns, so a job sheet is never lost.
