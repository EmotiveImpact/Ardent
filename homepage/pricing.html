Common questions

Do my engineers need signal on site?

No. Job sheets, checklists and certificates work offline and sync as soon as the phone finds a connection.

Can you move our existing data across?

Yes, free on every plan. Send us your customer list, open jobs and certificate history and we’ll import it before you go live.

Which certificates can we issue?

Gas safety records (CP12), EICRs and minor works, PAT, fire alarm (BS 5839) and unvented cylinder certificates, with more on the way.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans run month to month and you can add or remove engineers whenever you like.
