What Ardent handles for lifts businesses

  1. 01

    Entrapments first

    Emergency calls jump the queue and go to the nearest engineer, with the building told the moment someone is on the way.

  2. 02

    Maintenance rounds planned

    Regular visits are booked from each contract, grouped by area so engineers spend less time driving.

  3. 03

    Examinations on time

    Thorough examination dates are tracked for every lift, with defects raised as quotes for the building owner.

  4. 04

    A history for every lift

    Visits, parts and faults are kept against each lift, so the next engineer knows what happened last time.
